## Chapter 4: Prescription & Repertorization

---

### 4.1 Create Prescription

**Steps:**

1. Open case → Click **Add Prescription**
2. Select Prescription Type: **Chronic**
3. Choose Remedy:

   * Search: *Belladonna*
   * Potency Available: C30, 200C, 1M
   * Selected Potency: **200C**

**Dosage Details:**

* Dosage: 3 globules
* Frequency: Twice daily
* Duration: 7 days
* Instructions: Empty stomach, avoid coffee

**Dietary Restrictions:**

* Avoid coffee, mint, strong odors

**Follow-up Advice:**

> “Report after 7 days or if symptoms worsen”

Click **Save Prescription**

✔ Stock deducted automatically
✔ Prescription ready for print

---

### 4.2 Multiple Medicine Prescription

Used for **intercurrent or nosode prescriptions**.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add Medicine**
2. Select: *Sulphur 200C*
3. Click **Add Medicine** again
4. Select: *Tuberculinum 1M*
5. Define order:

   * Sulphur first
   * Tuberculinum after 15 days
6. Save prescription
